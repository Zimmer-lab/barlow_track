## Description

Fieseler, Lev, Madhusudhanan, Zhai, Schwartz, Zimmer (2025). bioRxiv preprint 10.1101/2025.11.10.687595, v1 posted 2025-11-12. The user's own paper (first and corresponding author), now in revision.

Self-supervised tracking of segmented neurons in freely moving *C. elegans*: a 3D CNN encoder trained with a modified Barlow Twins loss (feature-space redundancy reduction plus a new object-space term computed across all objects of one time point) embeds 3D crops of each object; objects are associated across time by clustering the embeddings, with HDBSCAN or a custom time-aware label propagation plus spectral alignment. Benchmarked on Zimmer, Flavell and Leifer lab ground truth (Table 1, 2; Fig. 3, S1), applied to new conditions without ground truth (Fig. 4, S2), and shipped with a napari/NWB GUI (Fig. 5).

Figures: Fig. 1 motion complexity; Fig. 2 algorithm design; Table 1 datasets; Table 2 benchmark vs published methods; Fig. 3 benchmark accuracy, proxies, generalization, training volumes; Fig. 4 novel conditions; Fig. 5 GUI; Fig. S1 displacement, clustering comparison, ablations; Fig. S2 more novel conditions.
