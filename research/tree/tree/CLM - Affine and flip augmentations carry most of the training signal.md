## Claim

Geometric augmentations alone (affine or 180° flip) train near-baseline networks, while blur, noise or elastic deformation alone are close to an untrained network.

## Rationale

Fig. S1f: Leifer 97.4%, Flavell 94.2% with a single geometric augmentation.

## Scope

Supports the Discussion's view that success rests on augmentations resembling the brain's real deformations.
