## Claim

A BarlowTrack network trained self-supervised on each recording, with label-propagation clustering, reaches 96.7-97.4% accuracy on ground-truth recordings from the Zimmer, Flavell and Leifer labs.

## Rationale

Table 2, central result of the paper; the first tracker reported to work across independent labs.

## Scope

Accuracy over segmented objects only; Flavell GT is algorithmic; one Leifer recording.
