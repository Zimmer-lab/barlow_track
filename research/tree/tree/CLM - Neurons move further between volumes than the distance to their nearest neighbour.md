## Claim

In freely moving whole-brain recordings from three labs, typical frame-to-frame neuron displacement is about one to three nearest-neighbour distances.

## Rationale

Direct measurement on ground-truth positions (Fig. 1c-d, S1a-c).

## Scope

Head neurons of young adult *C. elegans* at 1.7-6 volumes/s, as recorded by the Zimmer, Flavell and Leifer labs.
