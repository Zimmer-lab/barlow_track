## Claim

Training on the new object-space term alone gives the same tracking accuracy as the original Barlow Twins redundancy-reduction term or their mixture; accuracy is insensitive to the balance between them.

## Rationale

Fig. S1e and the loss-balance sweep (section 2.5). The paper states it directly: "instead of showing a large improvement via our novel loss term, we have shown that the original redundancy reduction loss term can be completely replaced without performance penalties."

## Scope

This is a negative result for the abstract's headline contribution (the new term), established on near-ceiling benchmarks.
