## Purpose

Test changes of the reference channel: the NeuroPAL background (dimmer, more variable, sparser reference promoter, as in the Flavell data) and a strain with an additional genetic marker that changes neuron neighbourhoods.

## Method

Fig. S2a-c (NeuroPAL) and S2d-f (extra marker): heatmaps, traces, coverage; S2g-i displacement and nearest-neighbour distance per condition.

## Results

Good performance by coverage and heatmap quality in both.

## Conclusion

Neither a dim, heterogeneous reference nor an extra marker prevented tracking with high coverage.

## Caveats

As reported in the paper; not rerun. No ground truth: judged by track coverage and heatmap/trace quality only, which Fig. 3d-f shows is a weak proxy on two of three labs. Number of animals per condition not stated (5 animals across all novel conditions, Table 1). No figure script found in the repository. Supplementary figure, digested to main points only.
